ABOUT MOMENTUM

Momentum is a gamified habit tracker built on the Solo Leveling system: every habit, daily and quest you complete earns XP and gold, damages the raid boss, and levels your hunter. Streaks raise your rank from E through S. Progress is stored locally in your browser — nothing leaves the device.

DOCS — QUICK START

Habits — tap + to bank a streak (+10 XP, +2 gold, 200 boss damage) or − to undo one.

Dailies — tick a checkbox for +20 XP, +5 gold and 500 boss damage.

Todos — one-time quests worth +50 XP, +15 gold and 1000 boss damage.

Boss Raid — Cast Ultimate spends 10 MP for 1500 damage; Revive Boss resets it to 10000 HP.

Character — the radar plots your five stats; the trend chart plots XP earned over the last seven days.

Reset — the header button wipes local state and reseeds the demo data.
