Quick start

HABITS

Tap + to bank a streak (+10 XP, +2 gold, 200 boss damage) or − to undo one.

DAILIES

Tick a checkbox for +20 XP, +5 gold and 500 damage. Untick to reverse it.

TODOS

One-time quests worth +50 XP, +15 gold and 1,000 damage, filed under Completed when done.

BOSS RAID

Cast Ultimate spends 10 MP for 1,500 damage. Revive Boss resets it to 10,000 HP.

CHARACTER

The radar plots your five stats; the trend chart plots XP over the last seven days.

SHORTCUTS

Press ? in the app for the full key map. Digits 1–9, 0, − and = switch tabs.
