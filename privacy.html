Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 October 2026 · applies to Momentum V5.0.0 Apex (web, desktop and mobile builds).

1. Data we collect

Momentum stores your habits, dailies, todos, streaks, XP, gold, achievements, hunter profiles and preferences in your browser's localStorage under two keys: momentum.v5.state and momentum.v5.prefs. That data never leaves your device. We do not collect, receive, store or have access to it.

2. How we use it

Your data is read by the application on your device to render your progress, compute streaks, award XP and draw your charts. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party, because there is no server component to transmit it to. There is no account system, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no telemetry of any kind in this build.

3. Third parties

Momentum requests assets from third-party content delivery networks. Those requests disclose your IP address and user agent to the provider in the ordinary way any web request does. They carry none of your progress data:

  • DiceBear (api.dicebear.com) — generates hunter avatars. Only the seed, hair variant and colour in the image URL are sent; nothing about your habits or progress.
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) — serves the Orbitron, Inter and Press Start 2P typefaces.
  • Tailwind CSS and jsDelivr — serve the utility stylesheet and the confetti library.

If a CDN is blocked or unavailable, the app degrades: avatars fall back to a placeholder and the app keeps working.

4. Your rights

Because all data is local, you exercise your rights directly. Open the Settings tab to export your entire state as JSON (or your history as CSV), import a previous backup, or clear all data with a double confirmation. Clearing your browser's site data for this origin has the same effect. There is nothing for us to delete on your behalf, because we never held a copy.

5. Cookies

Momentum sets no cookies and uses no cookie-equivalent tracking. The service worker caches static assets for offline use; that cache holds application files, not your progress, and it is removed when you clear site data.

6. Children

Momentum is intended for users aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and because no data is collected there is nothing to request the deletion of.

7. Changes

If a future version introduces cloud sync or any feature that transmits data, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described in the app's release notes. The date at the top of this page always reflects the current policy.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@example.com. Replace this address with your own before publishing.
